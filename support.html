Getting started

How do I open Picky on a page?

Click the Picky icon in your browser toolbar, or press Alt + Shift + M. The toolbar appears on the page and you can drag it where you want. Press the same shortcut again to hide it. Once it's open, press M, R, C, S or V to pick Measure, Responsive, Color Picker, Screenshot or Video.

The toolbar didn't appear. What now?

Picky can't run on browser pages such as the new-tab page, chrome:// and edge:// pages, or the extension stores. Clicking the icon there opens a short guide instead. Try it on a normal website. If it still doesn't show, reload the tab after installing, or remove and re-add the extension.

The shortcut doesn't work in my browser.

Another extension or the browser may already use that key combination. Open your browser's extension shortcuts page (edge://extensions/shortcuts or chrome://extensions/shortcuts) and set one for Picky. The toolbar icon always works.

Why does Picky need access to all sites?

Picky inspects whatever page you're on, so it asks to run on any site. It only starts when you open it, and the analysis happens in your browser. The Privacy Policy lists every permission and why it's needed.

Using the tools

How do I measure the distance between two elements?

Press M for Measure, click the first element, then click the second. Picky draws scale marks with the exact pixel distance. Press Escape to leave measure mode.

Do my live edits change the real site?

No. Edits are temporary inline styles in your browser and disappear when you reload the page. Nothing is saved to the website or sent anywhere.

Can I copy styles out of Picky?

Yes. Copy the selected element as CSS, and copy any color as HEX, RGB or HSL. Tailwind, SVG and JSON are part of Pro.

How do I preview my page on a phone or tablet?

Press R for Responsive. Pick devices from the list, which covers about 100 phones, tablets, laptops and desktops, or add a size of your own. Each one renders at its real viewport size.

How do I share a screenshot?

Press S, capture, then annotate or crop in the editor and choose Share. Picky asks first, then uploads that one image and gives you a link. Anyone with the link can see the image, and the link expires after 30 days. You don't need an account.

How long can I record video?

Press V, choose the tab, an area or the screen, and record. Clips are up to 30 seconds on the free plan and 5 minutes on Pro. At the limit the clip is saved automatically.

Plans and privacy

Is Picky free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use, with limits on some of them, such as 30-second video clips. Pro raises those limits. You can choose a plan from Options, then Account, in the extension.

Do I need to sign in?

No. Signing in is optional and only syncs your settings, such as your toolbar choices and theme, across browsers.

Does Picky collect my data?

No analytics, tracking or telemetry. Picky contacts a server only when you start it: sending a bug report, signing in to sync settings, or sharing a screenshot. The pages you visit are never uploaded. Read the full Privacy Policy.
